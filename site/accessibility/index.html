Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA on Agora, and we say plainly where we fall short.

What we do

  • Every page starts with a link that skips straight to its main content.
  • Links, buttons and other controls show a clear focus ring when you reach them with a keyboard.
  • Agora has one light theme, and its body text and links pass WCAG AA contrast on its paper.
  • When your device asks for reduced motion, the replay on How it works changes steps in place, without moving.

Known gaps

  • No full accessibility audit of Agora is on record. When one runs, what it finds is listed here with its date.

Tell us

  • A contact for accessibility reports is an owner decision that is still open, so this page lists none today. When one is named, it appears here.
