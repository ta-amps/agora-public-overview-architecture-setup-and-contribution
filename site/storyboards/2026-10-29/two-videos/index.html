Storyboard draft 1: two videos

The first storyboard, kept as history. It planned two videos. The current plan replaces both with one nonprofit story.

Draft 1, October 29, 2026, afternoon. Kept as it was written.

How to read it

  • Real It worked when this draft was written and could be filmed as it was.
  • Building Someone was building it. It was not ready to film.
  • Needed It did not exist yet.

Video 1: AI is smart. The way we use it is frustratingly dumb.

A developer asks a coding agent for a video made with code. On its own it stalls; connected, it finds fresh tools, and the work splits into small jobs many workers share. Target 2:30 to 3:00, wide 16:9 plus a tall 9:16 cut.

The making-of cut

A 60-second cut shows the Tessera event log of this video’s verbs, with every asset’s hash, and the human narration verb highlighted.

What must exist before recording

In order:

  1. The video seed pack renders, encodes and checks wide MP4 files. This pipeline is being built.
  2. One real tool-discovery run that stores recent repo and benchmark nouns. The seed packs for it are being built.
  3. A Herma shim rule for video-as-code requests that returns those nouns with their dates.
  4. The production itself queued as a graph of verbs on Tessera, so the board capture is genuine.
  5. Feedback nouns and a simple thumbs control, as in the feedback design.
  6. Taylor’s narration recorded, as a person’s verb, with captions from Voice Transcribe.
  7. Music and sound effects with checked licenses (public domain or owned), stored as nouns with their license details.

Video 2: Spare tokens build tools for good

A volunteer lends their agent a few spare minutes. Through Eranos it builds a small, tested kit for a nonprofit; another volunteer’s agent checks it, and a person signs off. About two minutes.

TimeOn screenNarrationStatus

  1. Shot 1, 0:00 to 0:15

    On screen
    Two facts, each needing a source before use: many people pay for AI they don’t fully use, and many nonprofits can’t afford the tools they need.
    Narration
    “What if your unused AI could build tools for people who need them?”
    Status
    NeededSources for both facts, or the facts dropped.

  2. Shot 2, 0:15 to 0:40

    On screen
    Real capture: a volunteer copies the starting brief from the Eranos site into their agent.
    Narration
    “A volunteer connects their own agent, with tokens they weren’t going to use.”
    Status
    RealThe site and its brief.

  3. Shot 3, 0:40 to 1:10

    On screen
    The agent claims a brief, “Intake checklist for a legal-aid hotline”. It builds the kit (a skill file, a prompt and synthetic test cases) and checks its own work.
    Narration
    “It picks a real need and builds a small kit, with tests.”
    Status
    RealFive example kits, on a local machine.NeededA hosted run, captured live.

  4. Shot 4, 1:10 to 1:30

    On screen
    A different volunteer’s agent reviews the kit (a builder can’t review their own), then a person releases it.
    Narration

    RealThe review rules are enforced.

    On screen

    Real

    On screen
    Close: the Eranos link and “Donate your spare tokens”.
    Narration

    Status
    Real

What must exist before recording

  1. A hosted volunteer run captured from start to finish: the building agent, the reviewing agent and the steward’s release.
  2. A source for every statistic shown, or the statistics dropped.
  3. A short, plain preview page for a kit.
