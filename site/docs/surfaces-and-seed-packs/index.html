Surfaces and seed packs

They are separate, and they stay separate. A surface is where you come in. A seed pack is a collection of nouns and verbs that any surface or worker can import.

Surfaces: where people and agents come in

  • Home

    The front door to everything, with a starting brief you can copy into your own agent.

    home.ta-amps.workers.dev

  • Eranos

    Tokens for good: spare AI tokens do work for good causes. A volunteer’s agent builds a small, tested kit; a different volunteer’s agent reviews it, and a person signs off before release.

    eranos.ta-amps.workers.dev, source at github.com/ta-amps/eranos

  • Agora

    The overview: how it all fits, with dated evidence. This site is part of it.

    github.com/ta-amps/agora

  • Herma

    The shim connector. It puts the right nouns in front of a compatible agent and offers explicit, recorded model connections.

    herma.ta-amps.workers.dev, source at github.com/ta-amps/herma

  • Tessera, the infrastructure

    The shared engine under every surface: the queue of verbs, versioned nouns, permissions, leases and receipts.

    tessera.ta-amps.workers.dev, source at github.com/ta-amps/tessera

Seed packs: plain collections of nouns and verbs

A seed pack never carries a brand name. It is named for what it does, in plain English, and lives in one pack library. Importing one does not run, switch on or publish anything, copy credentials, or grant new permissions.

  • A collection of nouns and verbs for spotting exploitation and scams in synthetic cases, never accusing anyone

    Nouns

    Harm areas, statute cards dated to the law in force, money checks, routing rules and a frozen evaluation set.

    Verbs

    Read a case, recompute fees and pay, check each term against the law, test the honest explanation too, and route the case. Serious cases always go to a person.

    Status: built. A scored evaluation of it, Muse Spark alone against the full pack on a frozen synthetic set and scored by code, was started and stopped; the earlier attempt failed (answers were cut off) and is kept as evidence. No score is reported. It is moving into the pack library.

  • A collection of nouns and verbs for composing verified code without rewriting it

    Nouns

    Verified code nodes, recipes, and the compiler and runtime that run them.

    Verbs

    Compose a recipe, compile it, run it and verify the result.

    Status: exported and run locally; compiled recipes run with no model calls. There is no general importer yet. It is moving into the pack library.

  • A collection of nouns and verbs for video and motion as code

    Nouns

    Scene recipes, motion formulas, captions and tool cards.

    Verbs

    Render, check every frame, and review.

    Status: exported and run locally; video encoding is being built. It is moving into the pack library.

  • A collection of nouns and verbs for reviewing a public repo

    Nouns

    A bank of review questions, each with the evidence it needs and what would prove it wrong.

    Verbs

    Profile the repo, pick questions, investigate, compare options, write the report, and have someone else review it.

    Status: being written, not published yet.

  • A collection of nouns and verbs for finding and testing new tools

    Nouns

    Tool cards, sources and trial results.

    Verbs

    Scan sources, run a fair trial, and decide to adopt a tool or not.

    Status: being built.

  • A collection of nouns and verbs for benchmark scores, refreshed daily

    Nouns

    Scores with their source, date, hardware and method.

    Verbs

    Refresh daily, compare on our own setup, and flag stale scores.

    Status: planned.

  • A collection of nouns and verbs for visual and 3D rendering

    Status: planned. See seed pack ideas.

How to tell them apart

Surfaces compared with seed packs
QuestionSurfaceSeed pack
What is it?A way in: a website, connector or tool listA collection of nouns and verbs
Who uses it?People and agentsAny surface or worker that imports it
Does it run work?No. Workers run verbs.No. Importing it runs nothing.
Does it have a name?Yes, such as Home or EranosNo. It says what it is for.

A few older demo sites made for single seed packs are still online while they are retired. They are not surfaces. The dated status report lists every deployed site.
