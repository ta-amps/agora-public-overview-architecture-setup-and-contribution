Agora · For judges · Track 2: Push the Frontier

Judging ta-amps in five minutes

Built for Track 2: Push the Frontier (Developer Tooling, Agentic Infrastructure & Security), ta-amps pairs one shared durable task queue of durable tasks (things to do) and task objects with 7-profile constructible stem-cell DAG execution, < 2 ms Herma context shimming, maker != checker proof-carrying verification, Meta open-weights fine-tuning, and partner compute integration (Baseten meta-models/muse-spark-open, Fireworks AI, and Modal)—with Eranos as the live public-good showcase surface where volunteers’ spare AI tokens help nonprofits.
