Propose a seed pack, or tell us what you think

Seed packs grow when people who know a field describe what it needs. Feedback shows us where this is still dumb.

Both forms are on GitHub and need a free GitHub account.

Propose a seed pack

A seed pack is a collection of nouns and verbs for one kind of work, in any industry, niche, topic or pipeline. You describe it; we review it with you in the open.

The form asks for:

A name in plain English
“A collection of nouns and verbs for …”, with no brand name.
The industry, niche, topic or pipeline
Who it helps, and with what kind of work.
The nouns it would contain
Reusable things: sources, rules, tools, code, recipes, datasets, checklists, examples.
The verbs it would run
Jobs a worker can claim, execute and report, such as “check each source for changes”.
How it stays fresh
Which verbs keep the nouns current, how often, and when something counts as stale.
Sources and licenses
Where the knowledge comes from and the terms for each source. “Unsure” is a fine answer.
How to check results
Tests, reference answers, a measurement or a reviewer who knows the field.
A way to reach you, if you like
A public handle or link. Never a private email or phone number.

An example proposal

Made up for this page, to show the shape.

Name
A collection of nouns and verbs for keeping food-safety rules current for small kitchens
Area
Food service, by region
Nouns
Rule summaries with their source and date, inspection checklists, a glossary
Verbs
Check each official source weekly, flag rules that changed, draft an updated checklist, have someone who works in a kitchen review it
Fresh because
A weekly check; a rule is stale after 30 days without one
Sources
Official public health pages, each with its own terms
Checked by
Every checklist line links to its source, and a person in the field reviews before release

A few rules, so proposals stay safe to share

  • Share only public facts and synthetic examples. Never paste credentials, personal data or anything private.
  • A proposal starts a review. It doesn’t run, install, admit or publish anything.
  • Packs are named for what they do, never with a brand.

Give feedback

Tell us what you looked at, what worked, what didn’t, and what you would change. If a page is wrong or out of date, name the page.
