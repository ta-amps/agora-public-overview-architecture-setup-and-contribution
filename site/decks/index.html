  • Overview and architecture

    AI is smart.
    The way we use it is frustratingly dumb.

    Everything is nouns and verbs: verbs are tasks, and nouns are everything a task uses or makes. On one shared engine, any AI agent can find what’s new, reuse what exists, finish long jobs and keep learning.

    ta-amps · Overview and architecture · ta-amps · Agora, the overview · October 30, 20261

    Overview and architecture

    Why AI work is wasted, what nouns and verbs are, with two illustrated examples, how they fix it with feeds, search and retrieval, shared queues and learning, the map, Tessera and Herma, and honest limits.
