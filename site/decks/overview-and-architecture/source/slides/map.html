How the pieces fit · the map

This is the map. Tasks and Task Objects are the largest pieces, at the top, because they are the system. Seed packs bundle them under plain names, and an agent kit is a seed pack made for an NGO’s agent. People and agents use and create them through two public surfaces: Eranos, the two-sided marketplace, and Agora, this overview. Everything runs on two pieces of infrastructure that nobody visits: Tessera keeps the work and the records, and Herma connects any agent to context and models.
