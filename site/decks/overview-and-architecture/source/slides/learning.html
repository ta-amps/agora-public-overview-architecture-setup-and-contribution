What works · learning, measured on synthetic tasks

Two small open Llama models were tuned in a loop built from nouns and verbs on one local GPU, scored by exact match on a frozen, synthetic tool-call test of 150 rows. Muse Glimmer 30B was tuned with QLoRA on free cloud GPUs for the same kind of task and scored on a fresh set whose hashes were registered before scoring; its pre-registered promotion rule passed all twelve checks, and nothing has been promoted or admitted yet. These are synthetic tasks: they show the loop works, not a general benchmark.
