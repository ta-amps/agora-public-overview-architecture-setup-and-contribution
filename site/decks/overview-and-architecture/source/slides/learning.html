What works · learning, measured on our evaluation tasks

Meta’s {{model:muse-glimmer-30b}} was tuned in a loop built from Tasks and Task Objects, with QLoRA on Kaggle’s free notebook GPUs, and scored by exact-match accuracy on our 150-task tool-call evaluation, a fresh test recorded before scoring: 93.3% to 99.3%. Its promotion rule, fixed in advance, passed all twelve checks, and nothing has been promoted or admitted. A second run tuned it to answer from memory, with nothing added to the prompt: 0.0% to 62.5% on a 120-task recall evaluation. These are our evaluation tasks: they show the loop works on one narrow job at a time, not a general benchmark.
