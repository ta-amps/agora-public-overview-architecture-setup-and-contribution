What works · records you can check yourself, published October 30, 2026

Two records anyone can check. First, the October 30 film: its production ran as sixteen claimed Tasks on a local Tessera store, each step recorded, and the published bundle includes a standard-library script that rechecks every record, checks that each input came from an earlier recorded output, and ties every published file back to its record. The store itself isn’t public and the voice is AI-generated, as the bundle says. Second, the hackathon itself: a deterministic scan read 705 of the 739 public repos in the event directory without running any of their code, and mapped their files to Task Objects and their entry points to Tasks. Mentions are text matches, not evidence of use: 415 repos reference Meta’s model API, and 30 show an MCP signal. The published summary has aggregate counts only, and nothing in it ranks or judges any team.
