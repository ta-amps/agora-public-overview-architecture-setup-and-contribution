What works · proof you can check yourself, published October 30, 2026

Two pieces of proof anyone can check. First, this film: its production ran as sixteen claimed verbs on a local Tessera store, each with a receipt, and the published bundle includes a standard-library script that recomputes every receipt, checks that each input came from an earlier receipted output, and ties every published file back to a receipt. The store itself isn’t public and the voice is AI-generated, as the bundle says. Second, the hackathon itself: a deterministic scan read 705 of the 739 public repos in the event directory without running any of their code, and mapped their files to nouns and their entry points to verbs. Mentions are text matches, not proof of use: 415 repos reference Meta’s model API, and 30 show an MCP signal. The published summary has aggregate counts only, and nothing in it ranks or judges any team.
