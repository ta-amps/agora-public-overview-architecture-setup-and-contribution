AN AGENT KIT IS A SEED PACK

Nouns and verbs for one organization’s agent

Nouns: instructions, skills, knowledge, connections, settings, working files, checks and tests

Verbs: a step map of what the agent drafts and what only a person does, plus building, testing and reviewing the kit

One noun can hold an agent’s whole setup: its harness, its model and the files in its working folder.

How the pieces fit · seed packs

A seed pack is just nouns and verbs with a plain name, such as composing verified code without rewriting it, or video and motion made with code. Packs are never surfaces and carry no brand. Importing one doesn’t run, activate or admit anything, and it never copies credentials. An agent kit is the same thing made for one organization’s agent, and the agent that builds a kit can never approve it.
