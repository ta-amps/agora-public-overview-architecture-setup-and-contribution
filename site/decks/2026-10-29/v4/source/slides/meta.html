Appendix · built with Meta

Meta’s models do the reasoning inside workers, and Meta’s open models are what the system learns into. We list only tools that are actually used; anything not yet used is marked planned. The Glimmer result is on synthetic tool-call data, and the same test set served two training runs, so it counts only after a fresh test. With 63 examples the gain was too small to trust; with 511 it was clear. More checked data mattered more than anything else.
