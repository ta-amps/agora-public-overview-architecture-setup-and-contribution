Seed packs keep themselves fresh

PLANNED

Benchmark scores

Nouns: scores with source, date, hardware and method

Verbs: refresh daily, compare on our own setup, flag stale scores

BEING BUILT

Finding new tools

Nouns: tool cards, sources and trial results

Verbs: scan sources, run a fair trial, decide to adopt or not

BEING BUILT

Video and motion as code

Nouns: scene recipes, motion formulas and tool cards

Verbs: render, check every frame, review

Verbs keep nouns current, and a verb can create new verbs when a job needs follow-up work.

Appendix · seed packs

A seed pack is nouns plus the verbs that keep them current. A benchmark pack doesn’t just store scores; its verbs refresh them daily and test claims on our own hardware. The same pattern covers tool discovery and video as code. Visual and 3D rendering is next.
