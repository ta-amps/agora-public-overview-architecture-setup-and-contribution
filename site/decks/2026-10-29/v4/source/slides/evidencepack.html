A hard test: a pack for spotting exploitation

A collection of nouns and verbs for spotting exploitation and scams hidden between ordinary-looking job posts, messages and payments, in synthetic cases.

  • Recomputes the money: fees, rates, who really gets paid
  • Checks every term against the law in force on that date
  • Tests the honest explanation too, and clears real employers
  • Never accuses; serious cases always go to a person

Appendix · seed packs

This is the hardest test of the idea. Spotting exploitation needs many sources, reusable checks like statute cards and arithmetic, and a long investigation that never accuses anyone. It is a seed pack: nouns and verbs any worker can use, not a separate product. Its scored evaluation produced no score because answers were cut off, and we say so.
