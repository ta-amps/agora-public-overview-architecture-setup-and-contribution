One answer, three parts

PART 1

Nouns and verbs

Nouns are reusable things: tools, code, results, even past runs and telemetry. Verbs are small jobs any agent can claim, execute and report.

PART 2

Shims

Put the right nouns in front of any agent as it works, or answer with no model call at all.

PART 3

Learning

Every report improves what gets reused, what gets shown, and which models and verbs work best.

Workers don’t just use nouns and verbs. They create both: new findings, and new follow-up jobs.

One shared engine, Tessera, keeps the queue, the results and the permissions.

The answer

Three parts. Nouns and verbs give AI work a shared shape: reusable things, and small jobs that are claimed, executed and reported, including failures. Shims deliver the right nouns, including past runs and telemetry, to any agent without changing it. Learning means every report makes the next job better. And workers can create new verbs, not just nouns, which is how big jobs break down into smaller ones.
