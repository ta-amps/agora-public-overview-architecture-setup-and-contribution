How a kit is made · example kit 05, as its reviewed record describes it

Here is one of the five example kits, as its record describes it. A neighborhood pantry that also runs a cold-weather shelter needs weekend volunteers briefed for every station. The agent drafts station cards and handoff notes; everything else, from conduct issues to emergencies to messaging volunteers, is marked person only, and agents never message volunteers on their own. It was built by one synthetic volunteer and reviewed by another.
