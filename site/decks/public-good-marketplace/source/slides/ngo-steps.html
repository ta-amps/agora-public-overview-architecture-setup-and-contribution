How to take part · for nonprofits, NGOs and public-good groups

A nonprofit’s path has four steps. It signs up for a scoped key, browses ready-made agent kits, types what it needs in its own words, and receives reviewed results or a local agent kit with a receipt for every step. It never has to choose a kind of request: the intake step checks safety rules and splits private-file work from public research. Since October 30 at 06:55 UTC, the kit catalog, sign-up and the request box are live: sending a first request gets the organization its own narrow key, and the request becomes one verb on the first queue until an authorized volunteer worker claims it and a second worker reviews the output.
