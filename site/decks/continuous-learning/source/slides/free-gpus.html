How it runs · free GPUs

You don’t need to rent a GPU to try this. Muse Glimmer 30B was tuned with QLoRA on Kaggle’s free notebook GPUs, two T4s: its 511-row run took 64 training steps in 83 minutes. Those notebooks aren’t public yet, but the run record lists every file’s hash. Llama 3.2 1B and 3B ran every round on one RTX 3060 with 12 GB: about 46 and 51 GPU-minutes, with no provider calls and no spending. Without a GPU, a stand-in backend runs every verb, receipt, report and decision on a CPU, so you can check the plumbing before you spend anything. One tick command plans and runs a round, and Tessera’s continuous-tuning guide has the rest.
