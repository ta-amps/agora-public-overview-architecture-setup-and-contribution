What we measured · Llama 3.2 1B and 3B, our evaluation tasks

Two small Llama models, the same frozen, 150-task evaluation and the same loop on one local GPU. The 1B went from 12.0% to 43.3% in round 1 and to 50.7% in round 3. Rounds 2 and 4 did not beat the champion, so the rule kept it. Round 1 gained 31.3 points, with a 95% interval of 22.0 to 40.7, and round 3 gained 7.3, with 0.7 to 14.7. The 3B went from 52.0% to 72.7% in round 1, a gain of 20.7 points with 12.0 to 29.3. Its round 2 scored 79.3% but was kept out by a schema guard. The first 63 owned examples were enough to stop the 1B writing typed values as strings, and its type correctness rose from 37.5% to 94.2%. Each configuration ran once, with one seed.
