Post-training tuning and continuous learning

The system keeps learning after it ships.

Small open models are tuned on checked examples, and a rule fixed before scoring decides whether one replaces its base.

ta-amps · Agora, the overview · October 30, 2026

This deck is about what happens after a model ships. Training made it, and then it froze: the tools, schemas and failures it meets afterwards never reach its weights. Here the system keeps learning. Learners turn execution reports into memory, path statistics and routing advice, and small open models are tuned on checked examples and kept only when they beat their base on a test they never saw, under a rule fixed before scoring. Every result in this deck comes from synthetic tasks, and nothing has been promoted or admitted.
