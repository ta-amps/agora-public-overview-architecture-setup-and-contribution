THE FIX

Re-tune on demand, promote after evaluation

Tune on checked examples of the job, and keep a tuned model only when it beats its base on a test it never saw, under a rule fixed in advance.

The problem

Training made the model, and then it froze. The tools, schemas and failures it meets after its release never reach its weights, so the same mistakes repeat. A bigger model isn’t the fix: on the same frozen test, Llama 3.2 3B beat the 1B, 52.0% to 12.0% exact match, but it wrote typed values as strings even more often, in 17.5% of its calls against 8.3%, and strict servers reject those calls. Even Muse Glimmer 30B, unchanged, answered 93.3% of a fresh test exactly right, and 96.7% of its replies were valid JSON. The fix is a loop we run on demand: we re-tune and promote only after evaluation, keeping only what beats the base on our evaluations.
