What we measured · Muse Glimmer 30B, synthetic tasks

The largest model we tuned is Muse Glimmer 30B, with QLoRA on free cloud GPUs and 511 synthetic training rows. It was scored on a fresh 150-row test built after training, with its hashes registered before scoring. Exact match rose from 93.3% to 99.3%, a gain of 6.0 points with a 95% interval of 2.0 to 10.7: 10 rows got better and 1 got worse. Valid JSON went from 96.7% to 100.0%, and no test row matched a training row. The promotion rule, fixed before scoring, passed all 12 checks, and a fresh reload of the adapter reproduced its replies on 8 of 8 rows. On the frozen development test it went from 87.3% to 98.7%. The rule said promote, but nothing has been promoted or admitted, and the adapter weights aren’t published.
