Why and how · tests and rules fixed before scoring

Everything that decides was written down before the results it judges. The frozen test is 150 rows over whole tool families that training never sees, pinned by its hash. Each round also draws fresh rows, and their hashes are recorded before any model sees them. The promotion rule was committed before the first training round, and a test pins it. Fingerprints keep look-alikes of test rows out of training. And the rule has said no to real-looking gains. In Llama 3.2 1B’s round 4, a new strategy lifted the development score from 61.25% to 76.25%, but the frozen test stayed at 50.7%. Llama 3.2 3B’s round 2 scored 79.3% but cut schema validity by 0.025, past its 0.02 limit. Muse Glimmer 30B trained on 63 examples gained 5.3 points on the frozen test, but its 95% interval started at 0.0. All three stayed out.
