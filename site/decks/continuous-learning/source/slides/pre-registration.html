FROZEN TEST

150 rows over tool families that training never sees, recorded once and never changed.

FRESH TEST

New rows each round, recorded before any model sees them.

THE PROMOTION RULE

Committed before the first training round, and pinned by a test.

NO LOOK-ALIKES

Fingerprints reject training rows that match a test row, its request or its template.

Why and how · tests and rules fixed before scoring

Everything that decides was written down before the results it judges. The frozen test is 150 rows over whole tool families that training never sees, recorded once and never changed. Each round also draws fresh rows, recorded before any model sees them. The promotion rule was committed before the first training round, and a test pins it. Fingerprints keep look-alikes of test rows out of training. And the rule decides both ways. {{model:muse-glimmer-30b}} trained on 63 examples gained 5.3 points on the frozen test, but its 95% interval started at 0.0, so it stayed out. Trained on 511 examples, it gained 6.0 points on a fresh test, with an interval of 2.0 to 10.7, and passed all 12 checks.
