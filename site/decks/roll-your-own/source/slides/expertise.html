Build Tasks and Task Objects for your area of expertise · your field, as a seed pack

Whatever your field, what it knows can become Task Objects and the work it repeats can become Tasks. Task Objects are the things it reuses: rules, checklists, templates, sources, datasets, code, tests and evaluations, each versioned and kept with its source. Tasks are the jobs it does again and again, such as researching, checking, drafting, comparing and reviewing, each with inputs, an acceptance check and limits, so any authorized agent can claim one. The fields on this slide are ideas, not claims about what exists or works today. A seed pack bundles a field’s Tasks and Task Objects under a plain name, and reading or importing one runs nothing and grants nothing.
