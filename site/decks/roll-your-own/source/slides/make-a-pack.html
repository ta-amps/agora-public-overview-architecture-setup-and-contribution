Make a seed pack

THE FORMAT

packs/<id>/source.json
  pack_id, title, version, summary
  nouns  slug, category, role, title, value
  verbs  inputs, checks, produces, bounds
packs/<id>/README.md
packs/<id>/code/   optional, with its tests

Plain-English names, starting “A collection of nouns and verbs for …”. No product or surface names.

BUILDER · python3 scripts/build_packs.py

Writes nouns.json, verbs.json and manifest.json, and adds the no_change, unknown and blocked outcomes to every verb.

VALIDATOR · make check

Offline: rebuilds every pack byte for byte, checks it against a snapshot of Tessera’s contract, recomputes worked examples and scans for secrets.

AGAINST TESSERA · make check-tessera

Runs every noun and verb through Tessera’s own models at a pinned commit, and writes the receipt that make check requires.

Start from the closest existing pack. Every verb needs at least one deterministic acceptance check.

Build nouns and verbs for your area of expertise · in ta-amps/seed-packs-library-of-nouns-and-verbs

A pack is one hand-edited file, source.json, plus a short README. Nouns have a slug, a registered category, a role, a title and a value; verbs have inputs, at least one deterministic acceptance check, the nouns they produce, a network mode and bounds. The builder writes the generated files, make check validates everything offline, and make check-tessera runs every noun and verb through Tessera’s own models and writes the receipt. Tested code can ship in the pack’s code folder with its own CI job.
