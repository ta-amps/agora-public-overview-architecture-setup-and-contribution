Turn findings into fixes

The findings-to-fixes pack: every error, issue, design flaw or annoyance becomes recorded, fixed and verified work.

1 · record-finding

Write it down at once, with evidence, even if it’s fixed a minute later.

2 · reproduce

Show it again at the exact version, smallest case first.

3 · triage

Merge duplicates, then set the kind, severity and sensitivity.

4 · propose-fix

Name the cause and one to three options, each with its guard.

5 · fix

Make the chosen fix, with a test that fails first.

6 · verify-fix

A different agent reruns the reproduction, before and after.

7 · add-regression-guard

Prove a guard fails without the fix and passes with it.

8 · close

Apply the close gate and write the closing version.

A fixer never verifies their own fix.

Failures stay on the record: every step writes a new version, and nothing is deleted.

Make and submit a seed pack · the findings-to-fixes pack

Whoever notices a problem records it immediately as a finding, with evidence. Other workers reproduce it, merge duplicates, propose and make a fix with a test that fails first, and a different worker verifies it and adds a guard that is shown to fail without the fix. Nothing is deleted, and a worker that notices a problem during any other verb proposes a record-finding verb rather than fixing quietly. These verbs are proposals; no worker has run them yet.
