Research what exists first: before anything new is built, a worker surveys our own Task Objects, then the library, then outside catalogs, and compares alternatives that always include reuse as is, adapt and do nothing; a person or a rule set in advance decides. Turn findings into fixes: whoever notices an error, issue or annoyance records it with evidence, other workers reproduce and fix it with a test that fails first, and a different worker checks the fix. Watch a feed: a scheduled scan Task turns each new item from a source into a Task Object and proposes follow-up Tasks, which any worker can claim under a lease. Workers only propose; the requester turns proposals into work under an explicit allowance, and writing a schedule activates nothing until an authorized schedule enqueues it. Today a daily check watches our own live services, and feeds for new repos, papers and model releases come next, with the tool-discovery and freshness-checks packs.
