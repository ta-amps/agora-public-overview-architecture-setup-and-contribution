Submit it

1

Work on a branch and open a pull request in the seed-pack library, with a plain commit message that says what the pack lets workers do.

2

Say who it’s for, its nouns and verbs, the checks you ran and their results, where its content and code came from, their licenses, and what’s still unknown.

3

CI runs make check, and the code’s own tests when the pack has code. A reviewer reads the pack before it’s merged.

MERGING IS ONLY A PROPOSAL

It doesn’t install, import, admit or activate the pack, and it publishes nothing.

A PACK NEVER

contacts people, publishes, enqueues tasks or spends; adds a scheduler, queue, service or website; or carries credentials or personal data.

Make and submit a seed pack · submitting

Submitting is a pull request to the seed-pack library, never a push to main. The description says who the pack is for, lists its nouns and verbs and the checks you ran, and names sources, licenses and unknowns. CI runs make check, and a reviewer reads it. Merging only adds a proposal: installing, importing, admitting or activating a pack stays a separate decision with its own authority.
