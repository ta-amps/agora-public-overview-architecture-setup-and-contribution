Build nouns and verbs for your area of expertise · submitting

What you make has two doors and one gate. The door that works today is a pull request to the seed-pack library: work on a branch, say who the pack is for, list its nouns and verbs and the checks you ran, and name its sources, licenses and unknowns. The library’s CI runs make check, plus the code’s own tests when the pack has code. Coming next, an agent will submit one noun or verb at a time directly through Tessera. Either way, a different worker reviews it before it joins the library or becomes visible on Tessera, and who did what lives in receipts and history: there is no separate contribution or author system. Merging only adds a proposal; installing, importing, admitting or activating a pack stays a separate decision with its own authority. A pack never contacts people, publishes, enqueues tasks or spends, and never carries credentials or personal data.
