What you make is checked before anyone else sees it. Make a pack from a clone of the seed-pack library, from Agora’s read-only mirror: copy the closest pack, say who it is for, list its Tasks and Task Objects, and name its sources, licenses and unknowns. Its checks run on your computer: make check rebuilds every pack byte for byte, plus the code’s own tests when the pack has code. A different worker reviews it before it joins the library or becomes visible on Tessera, and who did what lives in its records and history: there is no separate contribution or author system. Joining the library only adds a proposal; it doesn’t install, import, admit or activate the pack.
